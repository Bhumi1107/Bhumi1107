### DataProof Lab

What changes when you clean a dataset? Load the sample CSV, preview removing a duplicate, and compare the original and cleaned summaries side by side. You can see exactly which numbers change before deciding what to do with your data.

In the six-row sample, three cells are blank and one row appears twice. Removing the duplicate and filling the missing score with the median brings completeness from **90% to 92%**. Two blanks remain in the contact column.

[Open the demo](https://bhumi1107.github.io/DataProof-lab/) · [See the sample CSV](https://github.com/Bhumi1107/DataProof-lab/blob/main/demo.csv) · [Read the code](https://github.com/Bhumi1107/DataProof-lab)

### RepoSense AI

What makes a GitHub issue sound like a bug or a feature request? Try a sample issue to see its predicted label and the words that influenced it. Correct the label and the local Naive Bayes model learns from your feedback.

[Open the demo](https://bhumi1107.github.io/RepoSense-AI/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/Bhumi1107/RepoSense-AI)

### CareFlow Studio

How does one appointment change a department’s workload? Add a fictional visit, then open the weekly planner to see where it lands. Try booking the same doctor at the same start time to see the scheduling check in action.

[Open the demo](https://bhumi1107.github.io/Hospital-Management-/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/Bhumi1107/Hospital-Management-)
